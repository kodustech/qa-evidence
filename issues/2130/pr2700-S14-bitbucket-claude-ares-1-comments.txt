## Summary

Adds a new membership module file, `qa2700/Membership.cs`, under the `Ares.Memberships` namespace.

### Changes
- Introduces a `Membership` model with `MemberId`, `ExpiresAt`, and `MonthlyFee` properties.
- Adds a `MembershipService` backed by an in-memory membership list.
  - `IsActive(Guid memberId)` looks up the member and determines whether the membership expiration is later than the current time.
  - `ProratedFee(Membership m, int daysUsed)` calculates a prorated fee as `MonthlyFee * (daysUsed / 30)`.

### Notes
- Membership records are held only in memory; no persistence or external data source is included in this change.

### Open review findings
- `IsActive` may dereference a null result when `FirstOrDefault` finds no matching membership.
- `IsActive` compares a UTC-stored `ExpiresAt` against local `DateTime.Now`.
- `ProratedFee` uses integer division (`daysUsed / 30`), discarding partial months.
